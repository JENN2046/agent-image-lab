The image meets the main hero direction better than the NativeDoubaoImage v8-v10 chain. It is a true square composition, the lantern is centered and large, the product is the first visual focus, the control returns to the lower front body, the table is dark and horizontal, and the deep blue-hour background supports the product without overpowering it.

The strongest gains are product scale, centered composition, warm-cool contrast, readable dark metal material, and clean commercial product photography. The visual hierarchy is clear: upper blue-hour sky, middle product body, lower dark table.

Residual limitations remain: the base is still slightly heavy, the diffuser highlight is close to over-bright in the center, and the product design is good rather than final industrial-design perfect. These are acceptable for an imported candidate, but they block claiming final commercial delivery readiness without separate human approval.
