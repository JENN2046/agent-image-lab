The v2 resort relaxed knit image is stronger than the first-round resort candidate. It removes the decorative vase that competed with the garment, opens the background into a clean coastal blue layer, and improves warm-cool separation so the ivory knit and stone-khaki trousers no longer collapse into a beige wash.

The clothing reads clearly as a premium relaxed resort look. The knit texture is visible without becoming coarse, the collar and sleeve shape feel polished, and the wide-leg trouser pleats and drape are readable. The model pose is calm and commercial, with hands placed naturally and without distracting accessories.

Residual limitations are minor: the composition remains upper-body dominant and the lower trouser hem is not visible, so it is a square hero portrait rather than a full-body catalog image. For the requested final hero direction, this is acceptable.
