## Product Identity

The product is a premium skincare serum bottle. It should look like a refined beauty product for ecommerce or high-end product photography.

Locked structure: frosted translucent glass bottle with a clean dropper cap.

Do not reinterpret the product as a perfume bottle, medicine bottle, candle jar, beverage bottle, lotion pump bottle, or generic plastic container.

## Material / Texture

- Frosted or softly translucent glass body.
- Subtle internal depth, not muddy or distorted.
- Soft highlights along glass edges and shoulders.
- Clean dropper cap with credible collar and top geometry.
- Premium cap material, preferably satin white, warm silver, champagne metal, or soft neutral finish.
- No cheap plastic drift.
- No overly metallic bottle body.

## Packaging / Label Boundary

- Elegant skincare packaging.
- Blank or non-readable label zone is allowed.
- No readable brand logo.
- No fake text, random letters, watermark, pseudo-brand marks, or broken label.
- Label panel should support the product identity without becoming the subject.

## Scene Direction

- Clean premium beauty product still life.
- Straight-on or slight three-quarter front angle.
- Product remains dominant, approximately 60-75 percent of visual importance.
- Clean stone, ivory, warm-gray, or soft neutral surface.
- Subtle luxury background with gentle depth.
- No people, hands, flowers, spa props, boxes, or extra bottles by default.

## Commercial Image Goal

Create a premium beauty ecommerce hero image that emphasizes product identity, glass material realism, cap geometry, label discipline, and controlled reflections.

The result should feel commercially useful as a product prompt workflow target, but this brief does not mark any future image as commercial delivery ready.

## Key Visual Risks

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Glass / plastic material drift | Lock frosted translucent glass and soft edge highlights |
| Broken label or fake text | Require blank / non-readable label zone |
| Unrealistic reflections | Require controlled premium reflections |
| Liquid / bottle distortion | Require stable silhouette and subtle internal depth |
| Cap / dropper geometry errors | Lock clean dropper cap and forbid pump artifacts |
| Overdecorated luxury scene | Default to no props and a subtle background |
| Product scale ambiguity | Require dominant hero product scale |
| Logo / brand hallucination | Forbid readable logo, fake text, random marks |
| Background overpowering product | Require background to support, not dominate |

## Acceptance Criteria Draft

- The asset clearly reads as a premium serum bottle.
- Frosted or translucent glass material is credible.
- Dropper cap geometry is clean and symmetric.
- Label area is clean, minimal, and non-readable.
- Reflections are soft, controlled, and premium.
- Product is dominant and not buried by background.
- Background is subtle, clean, and beauty-oriented.
- No readable logo, no fake text, no people, no distracting props.
- No product shape drift, no perfume / medicine / plastic-container drift.

## Negative Constraints Draft

- No readable brand logo.
- No fake text or random letters.
- No broken label.
- No watermark.
- No people or hands.
- No overdecorated spa scene.
- No cheap plastic material drift.
- No metallic bottle body drift.
- No perfume bottle drift.
- No medicine bottle drift.
- No malformed dropper cap.
- No fused cap or pump artifact.
- No unrealistic reflections.
- No background overpowering product.
